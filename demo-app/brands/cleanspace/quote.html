Our Hiring Process

You deserve to know who's walking through your door. Here's how we select and train every member of our team:

  1. Values-based screening. We hire for character first: teaming up, serving others, being real, and making it happen.
  2. Group + one-on-one interviews. Twice weekly. Every serious candidate meets ownership directly.
  3. Background check & drug screen. Required before a working interview is offered.
  4. Paid working interview. Supervised by our training lead. Real work, real evaluation.
  5. 2-week paid training period. Followed by an official offer, a raise, and a welcome package.
  6. Quarterly reviews, raises & bonuses. We retain great people by investing in them. Retention means consistency for you.
